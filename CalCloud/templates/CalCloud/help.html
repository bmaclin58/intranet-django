FAQs

Where can I find pre-filtered asset reports?

The four cards at the top of the Dashboard open the Assets page filtered by Active, Inactive, Due, or Overdue.

Where can I view my service history?

The Historical Services Complete chart on the Dashboard shows In Lab, On Site, and Out Of Lab services over twelve months. This demo uses synthetic chart history.

Where can I view my assets that are out of tolerance?

The Dashboard's Out of Tolerance chart compares instruments received in tolerance, adjusted into tolerance, and returned out of tolerance.

Where can I view all of my assets and export asset reports?

Open Assets, enter your filters, and select Apply Filters. Export Report downloads every matching row across all pages, in the current sort order.

Are there more filter options?

Select Additional Filters to search Brand, Model Number, Serial #, Location, Sub-Location, Operating Range, and Condition.

Can I change asset details or request certificates?

This local demo is read-only. Asset details are available through View. Editing, certificates, and service submissions are available only in the original portal.

Where can I view changes to my asset details?

The ChangeLog page provides searchable example history. Use Reset to clear the grid settings or Export to download matching changes.

How can I contact a CalCloud representative?

Visit the CalCloud contact page for assistance.

For questions about the original service, visit Standard Calibrations. Help resources open on the original website.
